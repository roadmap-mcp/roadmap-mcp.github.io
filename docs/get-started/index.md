# Get started

Roadmap comes in two editions. Both run on your own machine, keep their data in
plain files, and work with the AI provider you bring.

| Edition | For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Lite](lite.md) | Small teams: compose contexts and serve them over MCP | To be released soon |
| [Pro](pro.md) | Teams and organisations: a shared Knowledge Base, git mining, every source | Available as a preview build |

To get going with Pro:

1. [Download and install](pro.md) it, and open it in your browser.
2. [Configure Git](git.md) so Roadmap can read your repositories.
3. Connect an AI client over [MCP](../reference/index.md), and read the
   [Knowledge Base](../knowledge-base/index.md) section for what to do next.
