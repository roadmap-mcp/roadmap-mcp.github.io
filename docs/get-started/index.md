# Get started

Roadmap comes in two editions. You install them yourself, on your own computer or
on a server for your team, and they work with the AI provider you bring.

| Edition | For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Lite](lite.md) | Small teams: compose contexts and serve them over MCP | To be released soon |
| [Pro](pro.md) | Teams and organisations: a shared Knowledge Base fed by Git, Confluence, Jira and Bitbucket | Available as a preview build |

To get going with Pro:

1. [Download and install](pro.md) it, open it in your browser, and create the root
   user.
2. [Add users](users.md), each with a role.
3. [Configure Git](git.md) so Roadmap can read your repositories.
4. Make an [API token](account.md#api-tokens), connect an AI client over
   [MCP](pro.md#connect-an-ai-client) with it, and read the
   [Knowledge Base](../knowledge-base/index.md) section for what to do next.
