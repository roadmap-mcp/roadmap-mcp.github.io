# Setup

Roadmap comes in two editions. You install them yourself, on your own computer or
on a server for your team, and they work with the AI provider you bring.

| Edition | For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Lite](lite.md) | Small teams: compose contexts and serve them over MCP | To be released soon |
| [Pro](pro.md) | Teams and organisations: a shared Knowledge Base fed by Git, Confluence, Jira and Bitbucket | Available as a preview build |

This section installs and configures the server. What each edition does is in the
[Lite edition](../lite/index.md) and [Pro edition](../knowledge-base/index.md)
sections.

To get going with Pro:

1. [Download and install](pro.md) it, open it in your browser, and create the root
   user.
2. [Add users](users.md), each with a role, and create a
   [workspace](workspaces.md) per team or project if you need more than one.
3. [Configure Git](git.md) so Roadmap can read your repositories, and
   [install a pack](../knowledge-base/packs.md) of personas and topic types, or set
   them up yourself.
4. [Install the plugins](plugins.md) you need on top of the bundled ones, if any.
5. Make an [API token](account.md#api-tokens), connect an AI client over
   [MCP](pro.md#connect-an-ai-client) with it, and read the
   [Pro edition](../knowledge-base/index.md) section for what to do next.
